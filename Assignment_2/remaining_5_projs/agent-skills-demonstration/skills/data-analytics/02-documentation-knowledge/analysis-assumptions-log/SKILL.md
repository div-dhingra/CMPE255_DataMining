# Analysis Assumptions Log

## Overview
Explicit tracking and sensitivity assessment of critical assumptions made throughout the analytical workflow.

### Target CRISP-DM Phase
**Phase 1: Business Understanding**

## On-Demand Context Pattern
1. **Request Minimum Viable Context**: Identify required input fields, business context, and operational constraints.
2. **Execute Analytical Workflow**: Perform structured investigation following standardized analytical protocols.
3. **Surface Assumptions**: Explicitly document data assumptions, boundary decisions, and confidence bounds.
4. **Deliver Structured Output**: Generate executive-ready summary tables, insights, and action plans.

## Practical Implementation Guide
- Maintain end-to-end lineage from source data to business metric.
- Reconcile aggregated metrics against baseline enterprise reporting.
- Convert empirical findings into prioritized business interventions with quantified ROI.

## Common Pitfalls to Avoid
- Silently accepting discrepancies between raw billing records and aggregated KPIs.
- Presenting technical statistical metrics without translating to dollar revenue impact.
- Omitting clear action owners and timelines in stakeholder deliverables.
