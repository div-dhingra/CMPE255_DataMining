### **Summary of the Process**
*We analyzed and performed an exploratory data analysis on a 'Top YouTubers' dataset, in order to interpret and derive engagement insights & statistics from various YouTube channels. This data was normalized, cleaned, and transformed to serve as usable training data for the model to predict YouTube performance trends based on engagement correlations and patterns from ~1000 successful YouTubers. The Colab and Chat cover thorough data preparation steps, applying K-Means clustering to categorize/group channels into distinct behavioral profiles. It also generated synthetic revenue data to cover income targets and perform supervised modeling techniques — evaluating various regression models (Random Forest, XGB, etc.) along the way.*
